Work With Us

Build Your Future in the Maritime Industry

At Lothal Marine, we believe our greatest assets are the people holding the welding shields, plotting the hull offsets, and coordinating the shipyard schedules. We foster an environment of continuous learning, mutual respect, and absolute safety commitment.

Whether you are an experienced marine structural architect, a certified pressure-parts welder, or an ambitious marine cadet, we offer a path to build a rewarding technical career. Our projects expose you to complex, multi-disciplinary steel construction and class society certifications.

We provide competitive compensation, comprehensive health coverage, continuous technical safety workshops, and performance-based career progression tracks.
