Company Profile

Delivering Maritime Excellence Since 2015

Lothal Marine Private Limited was set up in 2015 to provide engineering and manpower services to the ship building, ship repair and steel fabrication industry. In association with our partners, we have a legacy of delivering on outstanding, innovative marine projects across India.

As a testament to our growing operations, Lothal Marine Private Limited has recorded business of USD 0.75 Mn for the six-month period ended June 30, 2021.

With strategic yards and fabrication workshops on the west coast, we combine technical management with access to top-tier skilled personnel to execute complex marine retrofits, conversions, and fast-track steel building projects under classification society approvals.

Key Personnel

Our executive leadership team brings together decades of marine structural design, operations, and fabrication management experience.

JM

John Mathews (62)

Chairman and Managing Director

A graduate in Physics and a Postgraduate in Management from the Faculty of Management Studies, University of Delhi, Mr. John Mathews is one of the founder promoters of Master Shipyard Private Limited. In over fifteen years of its existence, Master Shipyard Private Limited has designed, built and delivered over twenty-five vessels of different types.

One of the major successes was the building of the 1200 T DWT dumb barge ‘TIR’, which was constructed and launched under IRS Class in just 70 days from keel laying to launching. The TIR was used for the carriage of a 750 T reactor from Cochin Port to Irumpanam and thence to Kochi Refineries Limited. A ninety-six TEU vessel, completely designed in-house and meeting the requirements of the newly developed River Sea vessel rules of the Indian Register of Shipping, has been fabricated under his direct leadership and guidance.

Mr. John Mathews has been closely managing the day-to-day activities and complete operations at Master Shipyard Private Limited till the setting up of Lothal Marine Private Limited.

Vision, Mission & Core Values

We are driven by a set of foundational principles that define how we construct, service, and conduct our business.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as a benchmark of engineering precision and safety in the medium-sized shipbuilding and repairs sector, promoting sustainable naval technologies.

Our Mission

To deliver robust, seaworthy vessels and meticulous dry docking repairs that meet structural class approvals, maximizing vessel lifespan and minimizing operational downtime.

Core Values

  • Integrity: Honest contracts and transparent material logs.
  • Quality: Standardized welding checks under class rules.
  • Safety First: Prioritizing drydock safety for all yard workers.
  • Innovation: Adopting modern hydrodynamic designs for efficiency.

HSE & QMS Policies

Commitment to Safety, Quality & Environmental Custody

Lothal Marine operates a strict Quality Management System (QMS) compliant with ISO 9001:2015 guidelines. Every hull plate weld, piping line, and paint coating thickness is inspected and certified before delivery.

Our Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) policy is built around the goal of Zero Accidents. We provide rigorous training to all welding, rigging, and painting crews. Safety barriers, harnesses, and mandatory PPE audits are standard across our operating slips.

We are dedicated to safeguarding the marine environment. We prevent chemical runoff and safely dispose of blasting waste, marine sludge, and heavy materials under national environmental laws.
