Overview

Pioneering Solutions in Marine Logistics & Shipbuilding

Established in 2015 and headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra, Lothal Marine Pvt. Ltd. has grown from a specialized engineering firm into a trusted partner in commercial shipbuilding, refitting, and ship repairs.

With an experienced team of marine architects, structural engineers, and skilled technicians, we construct and service medium-sized commercial cargo vessels, deck barges, tugboats, and custom pontoons.
