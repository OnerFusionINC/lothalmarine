Who We Are

Sustained Innovation & Engineering Excellence

Lothal Marine Private Limited was set up in 2015 to provide engineering and manpower services to the shipbuilding, ship repair, and steel fabrication industry.

The company, in association with its partners, has a legacy of delivering outstanding innovative projects, bringing robust naval design and skilled manpower to projects across India's west coast.

Learn More About Us

Core Capabilities

We provide full-lifecycle marine services, from conceptual ship design to complex repair, drydocking, and maintenance operations.

New Build Construction

Building custom barges, tugs, workboats, and specialized vessels tailored to strict performance and registry standards.

Ship Repair & Refits

Complete dry docking services including hull treatment, plumbing renewals, engine overhauling, and steel replacement.

Manpower & Services

Supply of highly skilled steel fitters, certified class welders, and supervisors for marine structural construction across India.

Recent Work

Lothal Marine, in association with its partners, has delivered on some outstanding innovative projects under strict class regulations.

2500 T 86 M long MS Class IX Oil Tanker

New Vessel Construction

Active new-build fabrication of an 86-meter long, 2500 T MS Class IX oil tanker. Construction progress features massive steel framing, deck plate installations, and class welder weld-outs on dry docks.

Barge Conversion to Acid Tanker

RINA Class (IACS) Retrofit

Complete conversion of a cargo barge to a specialized sulphuric acid chemical tanker. Project executed under strict RINA Class standards, involving advanced engineering retrofits.

The M V Al Arfat 48 M Cargo Barge

Docked at Reti Bunder, Belapur

Dry docking and positioning of the 48-meter cargo barge M V Al Arfat on the slipway for extensive survey and maintenance works.

Tank Fabrication & 60 MT Steel Renewal

Steel Fabrication & Tank Work

Extensive internal tank fabrication and replacement of 60 metric tons of marine grade steel plates carried out under class inspection.

Grit Blasting, Primer & Paint

Surface Prep & Marine Coatings

Full hull surface treatment involving professional grit blasting, primer coat application, and several coats of marine paint.

Launching on Balloons

Vessel Launching Operations

Successful float-out and slipway launching operations carried out using heavy-duty inflatable marine airbags (balloons).

Operational Yards

Ship Repair & Construction at Strategic Yards

Lothal Marine Private Limited carries out ship repair, barge building, and maintenance works at key dry-docking and slipway facilities on the west coast of India:

  • Chougule Shipyard (Jaigarh Port): Features a modern ship lift facility at Lavgan capable of docking vessels up to 20,000 DWT. Serving the Indian Navy, Coast Guard, and rigs of ABAN Offshore.
  • Maldar Shipyard (Belapur, Belapur Reti Bunder): Ideally suited for shallow-draft new builds, drydocking, conversion projects, steel renewal, and grit blasting.
